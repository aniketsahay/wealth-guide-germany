# Earning more: salary, benefits, side income

Headline figures (tax tariff, social insurance values, pension values, subsidies, allowances) were checked against sources on 11 November 2026. Rules of thumb and long-standing legal rules come from general knowledge and should be confirmed before anyone relies on them.

## Why income comes first

For most people under about 40 a pay rise moves wealth more than any investment choice. A permanent €300 net a month, invested, is worth more over 25 years than squeezing an extra half percent of return from a small portfolio. Every plan should ask what income could look like in three years.

## Salary

- **Know the market:** collective agreements (Tarifverträge), salary reports from job portals and professional associations, and openly posted ranges. Compare total compensation, not base pay.
- **Timing:** negotiate after visible results, at role changes, and when changing employer, which is usually where the largest steps happen.
- **Ask for a number,** backed by responsibilities and results, not by personal costs.
- **Compare on net.** Above the social insurance ceilings (€69,750 for health, €101,400 for pension in 2026) a gross rise keeps more of itself, because only tax applies.
- **Part-time, parental leave, sabbatical:** model the effect on statutory pension points and bAV as well as on pay.

## Tax-efficient employer benefits

Use these before asking for the same value as gross pay. Most must be paid in addition to existing salary to count.

| Benefit | Tax treatment |
|---|---|
| bAV employer contribution | Tax and social-contribution free within limits; see `retirement.md` |
| Vermögenswirksame Leistungen | Up to €40 a month, taxable but often overlooked |
| Sachbezug (vouchers, cards) | €50 a month tax-free. A threshold: at €50.01 the whole amount is taxed |
| Deutschlandticket / Jobticket | Tax-free |
| Company bike leasing | Private use taxed on a small fraction of the list price |
| Company car | 1% of list price a month; 0.25% for most electric cars up to a price cap |
| Meal allowance | Tax-favoured up to a daily limit |
| Childcare subsidy for children below school age | Tax-free |
| Health promotion | Up to €600 a year tax-free |
| Gifts on personal occasions | €60 per occasion |
| Employee shares | Tax-free up to €2,000 a year |
| Training paid by the employer | Tax-free if job-related |
| Home-office equipment on loan | Tax-free |

## Equity compensation

- **RSUs and options:** taxed as salary at the personal rate when they vest or are exercised. The employer withholds wage tax, often by selling part of the shares.
- **Later gains** after vesting fall under Abgeltungsteuer.
- **Start-ups:** § 19a EStG can defer tax on shares in qualifying young companies until sale or a time limit.
- **Concentration:** job and savings then depend on the same company. Sell vested shares on a schedule down to a small share of invested assets (for example under 10%).
- US-listed employers: check the W-8BEN and how the broker reports to Germany.

## Training

- Costs of training for the current profession are deductible as Werbungskosten without limit.
- Aufstiegs-BAföG supports master craftsman, technician and similar qualifications regardless of age.
- Bildungsurlaub: paid education leave of typically five days a year in most Länder.
- Employer-funded training often carries a repayment clause if the employee leaves early; check the terms.

## Side income

**Before starting**
- Check the employment contract: side work usually has to be reported and must not compete with the employer.
- Decide the form: freelance (freiberuflich: writing, teaching, consulting, IT, design and similar) needs only registration with the tax office; commercial activity (trade, online shop, crafts) needs a Gewerbeanmeldung.
- Complete the tax office's registration questionnaire (Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung) in ELSTER.

**Tax**
- Profit is added to other income and taxed at the personal marginal rate. Set aside 30–45% of profit for tax depending on the main salary.
- Simple cash accounting (EÜR) is enough for small businesses.
- **Kleinunternehmer:** no VAT charged or reclaimed if turnover was at most €25,000 last year and stays under €100,000 this year. Simpler, and cheaper for private customers; worse when costs carry a lot of input VAT or customers are businesses.
- **Gewerbesteuer:** only above €24,500 profit for sole traders and partnerships, and largely credited against income tax.
- **Tax-free allowances:** €3,300 a year for trainers, coaches and carers in non-profit bodies (Übungsleiterpauschale); €960 for other voluntary roles.
- Occasional private income under €256 a year is not taxed.
- A second employed job runs on tax class VI; a Minijob (up to €603 a month in 2026) is flat-taxed by the employer.

**Social insurance**
- Side self-employment next to a main job normally stays within the employee's existing health cover. If it grows beyond about 20 hours a week or earns more than the main job, the health fund may reclassify the person as mainly self-employed.
- Some self-employed professions are compulsory members of the statutory pension (teachers, coaches with one main client, craftspeople). Artists and writers may qualify for the Künstlersozialkasse.
- Platform sellers and landlords of holiday flats are reported to the tax office by the platforms.

**Ideas by effort**
- Low set-up: tutoring, freelance work in the main profession, paid speaking or teaching, club training roles.
- Medium: digital products, a niche online shop, renting out a room or parking space.
- High: rental property (see `property.md`), a business with employees.

Judge a side income by net profit per hour after tax, and by whether it builds skills or assets that last.

## Moving into self-employment

- Build 6–12 months of spending as reserve first.
- Arrange health insurance, disability cover and retirement saving before the first invoice; they are no longer automatic.
- Gründungszuschuss from the Agentur für Arbeit is possible when starting out of unemployment (discretionary).
- Voluntary unemployment insurance for founders must be applied for within three months of starting.
- Get a Steuerberater for the first year.

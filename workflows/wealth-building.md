# Workflow: long-term wealth building

For people with the basics in place who want a system that runs for decades.

## Foundation check
- Three to six months of spending in reserve.
- No debt above about 5–6% interest.
- Liability and disability cover; term life if others depend on your income.

## Step 1: fix the targets
1. Target number: annual spending in retirement × 25–30, minus the capital value of secured pensions.
2. Savings rate needed: run the projection for your horizon. If it does not reach the target, the levers are savings rate, working years and income, in that order of control.
3. Equity share that you will hold through a crash (see `knowledge/investing.md`).

## Step 2: automate the money flow
On payday, standing orders in this order:
1. bAV via payroll, up to the employer-funded part.
2. Altersvorsorgedepot (from 2027), up to the full-subsidy contribution if the money is not needed before 65.
3. ETF Sparplan in the Depot.
4. Safe part: Tagesgeld or Festgeld ladder.
5. Sinking funds for known costs (car, holidays, house repairs).

## Step 3: use every allowance, every year
- Freistellungsauftrag set and split; both partners' allowances used.
- Tax return filed; refunds invested.
- Children: Kindergeld invested in the child's or parents' Depot according to who should control it.
- High earners and self-employed: decide on Rürup contributions in the fourth quarter, when the year's income is clear.

## Step 4: raise the savings rate with income
Each pay rise: at least half goes to the Sparplan before lifestyle adjusts. Once a year, negotiate or test the market (see `knowledge/income-career.md`).

## Step 5: yearly review (one hour)
1. Net worth and savings rate against plan.
2. Rebalance if the equity share is more than five points off.
3. Check costs of funds and broker; check health-fund supplementary rate; re-quote insurance.
4. Update beneficiaries, will and powers of attorney after life changes.

## Step 6: later stages
- From 50: check the statutory pension record, consider buying back deductions, start shifting the next five years of spending needs into safe assets.
- Five years before stopping work: build a two-to-three-year spending reserve, plan the withdrawal order and health insurance status.

## Rules that keep it on track
- Never interrupt the plan because of a forecast.
- No single position above a tenth of net worth, except your own home.
- New products only if you can explain how they earn money and what they cost.

# Workflow: first investment

For someone who has never invested. Figures and example ISINs are in the "Core reference" section of `SKILL.md`.

## Before you start
- One month of spending in reserve and no overdraft or card debt.
- Privathaftpflicht in place.
- Money you will not need for at least five to ten years.

## Week 1: set up
1. Open a Tagesgeld account for the emergency fund if you do not have one. Set a standing order until it holds three months of spending.
2. Choose a broker: German tax handling, a free Sparplan on a global equity ETF, deposit protection for the cash account. Compare with a current test from Finanztip or Stiftung Warentest.
3. Open the Depot (video or app identification, ten minutes).
4. Set the Freistellungsauftrag at the broker. If you have interest income elsewhere, split it.

## Week 2: choose and start
1. Pick one global equity ETF (all-world, accumulating is fine). Check the current cost on justetf.com and that it is Sparplan-eligible at your broker.
2. Start a monthly Sparplan dated two or three days after payday. Any amount works; a tenth of net income is a good first target.
3. Decide the safe part: with a balanced profile, keep roughly a third of long-term savings in Tagesgeld or Festgeld.

## Month 2: take the free money
1. Ask HR: is there an employer contribution to a bAV? Are VL paid? Sign up for both if the employer pays.
2. From 2027: open an Altersvorsorgedepot and pay in at least the amount that earns the child bonus or the first subsidy tier.

## Every January
- Leave some cash in the broker account for the Vorabpauschale debit.
- Raise the Sparplan by the amount of any pay rise.
- Check the Freistellungsauftrag.

## Every December
- If gains are large and distributions did not use the allowance, consider selling and rebuying enough to use it.
- Request a loss certificate by mid-December only if you hold accounts at several banks with losses at one.

## When markets fall
Keep the Sparplan running. Do not sell. This is the plan working, not failing.

## Done when
Emergency fund full, Sparplan running, employer money claimed, allowance set.

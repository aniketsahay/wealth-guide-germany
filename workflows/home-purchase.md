# Workflow: buying a home to live in

Figures (transfer tax by Land, KfW programmes) are in the "Core reference" section of `SKILL.md`. Rules are in `knowledge/property.md`.

## 3–5 years before: prepare
1. Decide rent or buy honestly with the comparison in `knowledge/property.md`.
2. Set the budget: price range from an affordable monthly payment at today's rates, tested two to three points higher.
3. Equity target: purchase costs for your Land plus at least a tenth of the price, with the emergency fund on top.
4. Keep equity for a purchase within five years in safe assets: Tagesgeld, Festgeld ladder. Not in equities.
5. Clear consumer debt and tidy the Schufa record.
6. Check subsidies early: KfW family programmes, regional grants, the pension-subsidy route for housing.

## 6–12 months before: get ready to bid
1. Collect documents: three payslips, last tax assessment, proof of equity, Schufa data copy, existing loan statements.
2. Get a financing indication from a broker and a direct bank.
3. Learn the local market: prices per square metre, price-to-rent ratio, time on market.

## When you have found a property
1. Documents: land register extract, floor plans, energy certificate; for flats the declaration of division, three years of owners' meeting minutes, service-charge statements and the reserve fund balance.
2. Inspection with a surveyor for older buildings.
3. Final financing: compare at least three offers on effective rate, fixed period, special repayments, right to change the repayment rate, commitment fees. Apply for KfW loans before signing.
4. Notary draft: you are entitled to two weeks to read it. Ask questions before the appointment.

## After signing
1. Pay transfer tax when the assessment arrives; the purchase price when the notary confirms the conditions.
2. Insurance from handover: buildings insurance including natural hazards, adjust contents and liability cover; term life for both borrowers.
3. Start an upkeep reserve: roughly one to one and a half percent of the property value per year.
4. Rebuild the emergency fund to six months.

## Each year after
- Use special repayments when the mortgage rate is above what safe savings earn.
- Claim tradespeople and household services in the tax return.
- Three years before the fixed period ends, start watching follow-on rates; note the date when the ten-year termination right opens.
